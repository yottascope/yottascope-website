The game

A million-dollar problem.

Pomegranate Corp offers you a simple deal: enter an abandoned school, collect corporate documents, and walk away with one million dollars.

The problem is that the school isn't really a treasure hunt. The entire challenge is designed around getting you inside.

The Reality

MARKET POSITIONING

Market Comps & Target Audience

EFS1 targets fans of atmospheric, story-driven environmental horror and corporate satire. Positioned for players who enjoy games like Lethal Company, Phasmophobia, and Pacific Drive, combining narrative mystery with diegetic gameplay mechanics.

Why EFS1?

The fear isn't just what's inside.

Gameplay

You decide what to believe.

The core experience is exploration, environmental storytelling, investigation, and making the right call when the evidence starts contradicting the company.

Bad Ending

Trust Pomegranate.

Follow the company's instructions, reach the final zone, accept the physical check, and drink the celebratory coffee. The company's plan succeeds.

True Ending

Call for help.

Use the Pomephone P1 Max E, contact emergency services using 911 or 112 depending on region, report the illegal activity, and escape the school alive.

Dead Ending

Don't survive.

The player is caught by the sludge monsters and dies before escaping the school.
